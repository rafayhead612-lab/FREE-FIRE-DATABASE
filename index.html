Headshot Guide

Learn drag-shot technique, sensitivity, crosshair placement and movement tips for improving headshots.

  • Use suitable General sensitivity.
  • Keep your crosshair near the enemy's upper body.
  • Practice short upward drag movements.
  • Use Training Mode to improve consistency.
🎮

HUD Settings Guide

Build a comfortable HUD according to your fingers, device size and playstyle.

  • Keep the fire button easy to reach.
  • Place movement controls where your thumb is comfortable.
  • Avoid covering important information.
  • Practice the HUD before using it in ranked matches.
🔫

Weapon Guide

Learn about weapon categories and choose weapons according to your playstyle.

  • Use SMGs for close-range fights.
  • Use ARs for versatile combat.
  • Use shotguns when you are comfortable with close-range combat.
  • Use sniper weapons for long-range fights.
📱

Device & FPS Guide

Improve gameplay by using stable FPS, suitable graphics and appropriate sensitivity.

  • Keep your game updated.
  • Close unnecessary background apps.
  • Use stable FPS whenever possible.
  • Choose sensitivity according to your device.
